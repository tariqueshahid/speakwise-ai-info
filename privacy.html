Privacy Policy

App: SpeakWise AI (com.speakwiseai.fresh)  ·  Version reviewed: 2.2.0-rc1
Project: SpeakWise AI, independent English-learning project
Effective date: 12 November 2026
Privacy contact: speakwiseai.support@gmail.com

This policy describes the current reviewed Android app and the invitation-only pilot. Optional speech recognition may use a third-party Android speech provider; please read Section 2 before enabling microphone access.

1. Learning progress stored on your device

SpeakWise AI offers English vocabulary, lessons, games, stories, speaking and listening practice. The reviewed version does not require creating a SpeakWise AI account. Learning data such as an optional display name, language settings, saved vocabulary, XP, achievements, completed activities and exercise history is stored locally in the app on your device. The reviewed version has no developer-operated cloud progress sync service.

2. Microphone, speech recognition and text-to-speech

If you choose to practise speaking, the app asks for Android microphone permission. Speech recognition is performed by the speech recognition service selected on your Android device. Depending on your device, that service may transmit audio or recognized text to its provider for processing. The provider's own privacy, storage and retention rules may apply. The app developer does not control those external provider practices. You can deny or revoke microphone permission in Android settings and continue activities that do not require recording. Pronunciation and listening features may use your Android device's text-to-speech service.

3. Optional JSON export and restore

You may choose to export and restore learning progress using Android's file picker. An exported JSON file can include your learning history and any optional display name. Exports are not encrypted by SpeakWise AI. The location you choose to save a file—including a cloud service, if you select one—may have separate data policies. Keep exported files private and delete them from their saved location when no longer needed.

4. Analytics, advertisements and internet access

The inspected version of the Android app does not contain an embedded advertising or analytics SDK, a developer-hosted backend account service, or a direct Android Internet permission. Learning lessons are packaged with the app. However, Android speech or text-to-speech providers are separate components and may use their own connectivity and data handling.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on the device until you remove it using available app reset controls, clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not automatically delete JSON exports that you saved elsewhere. The current app does not run a cloud learner account that would need separate account deletion.

6. Security

The app uses Android app storage for local learning state and limits WebView network content in the reviewed build. Manual backup exports are unencrypted files, and device-level security and third-party speech services have their own protections and risks. No application or service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Intended audience

The current invitation-only pilot is intended for adults aged 18 or older. The broader audience and any age-specific protections must be assessed before a public app-store release.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app's features, third-party services or publication plans change. For questions about privacy, data handling or a request related to local app data, contact speakwiseai.support@gmail.com. We can explain on-device deletion steps but cannot remotely erase data stored only on your own device or third-party provider systems.

Scope note: This policy reflects a review of the existing v2.2.0-rc1 app source, not a device-by-device audit of all third-party Android speech service providers.
